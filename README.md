# Business Performance & Sales Analysis Using Tableau

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the given sales dataset using **Tableau Public** to identify important business patterns, trends, comparisons, and relationships. An interactive dashboard was created to present the findings clearly and support data-driven decision-making.

## Visualizations

The dashboard contains four visualizations:

1. **Sales by Category** – Compares sales performance across different product categories.
2. **Sales by Region** – Analyzes sales performance across different regions.
3. **Monthly Sales Trend** – Shows how sales change over time.
4. **Profit by Sub-Category** – Identifies profitable and less profitable product sub-categories.

## Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes two interactive filters:

* **Region**
* **Category**

These filters allow users to explore specific parts of the dataset and understand business performance in greater detail.

## Key Findings

* Sales performance varies across different product categories.
* Regional sales performance is not uniform, with some regions performing better than others.
* Profitability differs significantly between sub-categories, showing that high sales do not always result in high profit.

## Suggestions & Recommendations

* Focus marketing and inventory efforts on high-performing categories and regions.
* Review low-profit sub-categories and analyze pricing, discounts, and costs to improve profitability.

## Conclusion

The analysis shows that business performance depends on multiple factors, including **category, region, time, and product profitability**. The interactive Tableau dashboard makes it easier to identify strong and weak areas and supports better business decision-making.
